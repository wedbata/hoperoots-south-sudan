About Us

A South Sudanese organization, built by South Sudanese staff.

Since 2015 we have worked alongside communities in Warrap, Tonj, and Aweil — designing programs with them, delivering them with local teams, and handing them over to community ownership.

Our History

A decade of locally led programming.

From a single clean-water pilot in Gogrial to multi-state programming across three regions.

2015

Founded in Juba

HopeRoots South Sudan is registered as a national NGO by a group of South Sudanese development professionals and community leaders. First borehole drilled in Gogrial West.

2017

First institutional donor

First multi-year grant received from a European institutional donor. Team expands to 12 staff across Juba and Gogrial.

2019

Girls' Education in Tonj

Launched our first education-focused program, constructing girl-friendly school WASH facilities and training female mentors.

2021

EU co-funded Clean Water for Warrap

Co-financed by the European Union, the Clean Water for Warrap project begins — our flagship community-led WASH program targeting 42 boreholes.

2023

Emergency Food Security in Aweil

Mobilised within 60 days to deliver cash-for-work and seed distribution reaching 4,200 households facing acute food insecurity.

2025

42 boreholes · 28,000+ people reached

Cumulative reach exceeds 28,000 people with clean water. 96% of boreholes commissioned since 2021 remain functional at 12-month mark.

Our Vision

A South Sudan where every community owns the infrastructure that serves it.

We envision rural communities across South Sudan with reliable access to clean water, safe sanitation, and the local institutions to maintain them — designed and led by the people who use them every day.

Our Mission

Deliver accountable, community-owned WASH and humanitarian programming across South Sudan.

We work alongside rural communities to design and deliver water, sanitation, hygiene, education, and food-security programs — built to outlast our involvement, transparently reported to donors, and grounded in the leadership of local staff.

What We Stand For

Four values guide every decision we make.

Accountability

We publish every dollar. We report to communities and donors with the same candour. We commission independent evaluations for every major project.

Community Ownership

Every borehole we drill is handed to a trained community WASH committee. Every school we build is governed by its parent-teacher association. We design for handover from day one.

Local Leadership

Our staff are South Sudanese. Our board includes community representatives. We invest in training, not parachuting in consultants — because durable change is led by the people who will live with it.

Measurable Outcomes

Every project has a results framework agreed with donors before launch. We measure, we adapt, and we report what worked and what didn't — honestly.
