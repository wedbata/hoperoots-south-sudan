Beyond the numbers

What the data means for families.

3 hours Returned to every girl, every day

Before a borehole, women and girls in our target communities walked an average of 3 hours per day to collect water. That time now goes to school, childcare, and income-generating work.

41% Increase in girls' school attendance

In target schools under the completed Girls' Education in Tonj project, girls' attendance rose 41% over the project lifetime after safe, girl-friendly WASH facilities were introduced.

96% Boreholes still functional at 12 months

Independent verification in 2024 found 96% of boreholes commissioned since 2021 still functional at the 12-month mark — well above the sector benchmark of 80%.
