Flagship Project

Clean Water for Warrap

[ Image · Borehole drilling in progress, Gogrial East ]
Active 2021 – 2026 · 42 sites · Warrap State

Bringing clean water within 1.5 km of every household in Gogrial.

Clean Water for Warrap is our flagship community-led WASH program. Across Gogrial East and Gogrial West, we are drilling and commissioning 42 boreholes, training 38 community WASH committees, and establishing the local supply chains needed for long-term maintenance.

Each borehole is handed over to a trained committee with a 5-year maintenance plan, a stocked spare-parts kit, and a transparent tariff system managed locally.

Target reach: 28,000+ people with reliable clean water within 30 minutes' walk.
Sustainability: 96% of boreholes functional at 12-month mark (independent verification, 2024).

How We Work

The same five steps, on every project.

1 · Co-design with the community

We begin every project with multi-day community consultations in each target payam.

2 · Donor-approved results framework

Every project has SMART indicators agreed with the funder before launch.

3 · Local implementation

South Sudanese staff lead delivery, supported by community WASH committees at every site.

4 · Continuous M&E

Baseline, midline, endline surveys plus quarterly monitoring against the results framework.

5 · Community handover

Infrastructure is handed to community-owned governance structures with a long-term support plan.

6 · Transparent reporting

Annual reports, financial statements, and independent evaluations published publicly.
