Other Ways to Give

Beyond one-time donations.

Monthly Giving

Join our community of recurring donors. Monthly gifts give us predictable funding — the kind that lets us plan borehole construction years in advance.

Corporate Partnerships

We work with companies whose ESG commitments align with ours. Co-branded campaigns, employee giving, and cause-marketing partnerships available.

Legacy Giving

Leave a lasting legacy. A gift in your will can fund a named borehole, school, or scholarship in your honour — remembered for generations.

Volunteer

Lend your skills.

We accept a small number of skilled volunteers each year — typically in engineering, M&E, communications, and finance. Field placements are based in Juba and Warrap.

Stay Informed

Quarterly field updates.

Four times a year we share verified outcomes, site photographs, and stories from the communities we work with. No spam, ever.
