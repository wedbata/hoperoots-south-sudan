Our Mission

Locally led. Donor-accountable. Community-owned.

HopeRoots South Sudan is a Juba-registered national NGO founded in 2015. We deliver WASH, education, and food-security programming across Warrap, Tonj, and Aweil — designed with the communities we serve and built to last beyond our involvement.

Our staff are South Sudanese. Our board includes community representatives. Our budgets are published. We design every project so that local WASH committees own, operate, and repair the infrastructure we hand over.

Where We Work

Active projects across three states.

Each project is community-co-designed, donor-funded, and independently evaluated.

[ Borehole site photo ]
Active Warrap State

Clean Water for Warrap

42 boreholes across Gogrial East and West. Each site is handed over to a trained community WASH committee with a 5-year maintenance plan.

EU Funded by European Union
[ Classroom photo ]
Completed Tonj, Warrap

Girls' Education in Tonj

Constructed 4 girl-friendly school latrines and trained 60 female mentors. Girls' attendance rose 41% over the project lifetime.

UN Funded by UNICEF
[ Food distribution photo ]
Ongoing Aweil, Northern Bahr el Ghazal

Emergency Food Security in Aweil

Cash-for-work and seed distribution reaching 4,200 households facing acute food insecurity. Integrated with WASH to protect public health.

WFP Funded by WFP / USAID
